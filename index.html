What a thesis has to get through

Before Tibeb names a single company, the thesis and its evidence pass eight checks. They are the same for everyone, and you can see where yours stands at every step.

The checkWhat it takesWhy it is there

  1. Falsifiable thesis

    A yes-or-no question, a resolve-by date, and what you would expect to see if it is true and if it is false.

    If nothing could prove it wrong, there is nothing to research.

  2. Evidence depth

    At least 20 sources from 6 or more independent publishers.

    Two articles from one outlet count as one voice.

  3. Argued both ways

    At least 5 sources for the thesis and 5 against it.

    The case against is gathered on purpose, not left to chance.

  4. Sources verified

    At least 80% of the sources carry a link that was checked.

    Deep research keeps a source only if it finds the real link. What it cannot find, it drops.

  5. Primary sources

    At least 3 filings, datasets, papers or regulator documents.

    The thing itself, not someone's summary of it.

  6. Catalyst on the calendar

    At least 1 dated event that could settle the question or move it.

    A thesis needs a day it can be wrong on.

  7. Committee review

    A committee of AI analysts argues the thesis from the evidence on file, and a chair writes the memo.

    Each analyst commits to a probability. None of them recommends a trade.

  8. Verdict on file

    One probability that the thesis resolves yes, with a range around it.

    The companies named after it follow from the verdict, and each one has to cite sources from your ledger.

Change the question and the review, the verdict and the exposure go stale. They have to be earned again for the thesis you are actually holding.

It stops at research

The terminal ends at the companies exposed to the thesis: how it reaches each one, in which direction, and how much of the business turns on it, with the sources behind every line. What you do with that is your call.
